## Scope and decision

This pass reviews whether Rookery's rendered dashboard, alerts, action states, intent states, freshness, notices, disabled controls, focus, and narrow-width layouts communicate operational severity truthfully and accessibly. It covers text plus color semantics, contrast, keyboard focus, reduced motion, overflow, and the newly separated disconnected versus loaded-empty states.

Backend truth and remote behavior are outside this pass except where their state labels are rendered. The frontend-product workflow owns rendered verification; no visual redesign or generated imagery is justified.
